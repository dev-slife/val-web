Privacy Policy

Privacy with VAL

It's crucial to keep track of what data you share and what information is being collected. To keep as much of your data private, VAL will only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve your experience.

Information we Collect

Error Reports

Regardless if you have an account or not, we collect error information to help pinpoint issues in our code as you converse with VAL. Not all errors you encounter will be collected as data, only more critical ones such as, poor math calculations, or server-level bugs will be considered. The details provided to us when VAL encounters an error is as follows:

  • The type of error VAL ran into
  • The error message associated
  • The message given to VAL and
  • The type of action that was attempted

Account Data

If you choose to register for an account and decide to enable conversation history, we will collect all of your previous conversations with VAL. Please do not provide or enter any sensitive or personal information! We will never ask for your password or other sensitive info other than from the official login or register pages!

We Value your Data

Your data is never sold to third parties and we keep your data encrypted with industry standard methods to protect it. By using VAL, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
