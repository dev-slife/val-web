What Is VAL?

VAL (Virtual Arithmetic Luminary) is an AI-powered math assistant designed to help students, educators, and curious minds tackle mathematical problems of all kinds. Whether you're solving simple equations, exploring calculus concepts, or working through word problems, VAL is here to guide you step by step. Start a conversation at any time to get clear explanations, worked examples, and personalized support tailored to your level and goals.
